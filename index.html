About Me

Welcome to my website! I am Syeda Jahirin Aktari, a Ph.D. candidate in Economics (ABD) at Southern Illinois University Carbondale.

My research and teaching interests include microeconomics, development economics, industrial organization, and causal inference, with a particular focus on the effects of education policy on political participation and gender-attitude outcomes, as well as spectrum-sharing market design, information asymmetry, and socioeconomic welfare.

I have been involved in the field of economics for nearly a decade through both academic study and professional work. During this period, I have gained experience conducting rigorous empirical analyses to address real-world policy issues using R, Stata, and EViews. My current work explores the effects of higher-education policy on long-term political participation and gender-attitude outcomes.

My teaching experience demonstrates broad on-the-job training in both core and elective economics courses, including microeconomics, macroeconomics, health economics, urban economics, environmental economics, and the history of economic thought. I have taught in both in-person and online teaching modalities. Additionally, I have experience assisting with several management courses.
